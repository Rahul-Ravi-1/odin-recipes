Gochujang Chicken

Ingredients

  • 0.5 - 1 onion diced
  • 1 tbps gochugaru (Korean Chili Powder)
  • 1 tbsp gochujang
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp oligo syrup
  • 1 tbsp mirin
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp minced garlic

Instructions

  1. Get a mixing bowl or some type of container
  2. Put all ingredients above into bowl
  3. Mix it!
  4. Put your chicken or even any other desired meat and let it marinate
  5. Should be ready to cook and there you have it!
