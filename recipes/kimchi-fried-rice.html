Kimchi Fried Rice

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice (preferably day-old)
  • 1 cup chopped kimchi
  • 1/4 cup kimchi juice
  • 1/2 onion, diced
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp gochujang
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 egg (optional)
  • Green onions for garnish

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a pan over medium heat.
  2. Sauté onions until soft.
  3. Add kimchi and cook for 2–3 minutes.
  4. Add rice and mix well.
  5. Stir in soy sauce, kimchi juice, and gochujang.
  6. Cook until everything is heated through.
  7. Top with a fried egg and green onions.
