Bulgogi Beef

Ingredients

  • 1 lb thinly sliced beef (ribeye or sirloin)
  • 1/2 onion, sliced
  • 2 green onions, chopped
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp minced garlic
  • 1 tsp black pepper

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, mix soy sauce, sugar, honey, sesame oil, garlic, and pepper.
  2. Add sliced beef and onions to the bowl.
  3. Mix well and marinate for at least 30 minutes.
  4. Heat a pan over medium-high heat.
  5. Cook beef until fully done and slightly caramelized.
  6. Top with green onions and serve with rice.
